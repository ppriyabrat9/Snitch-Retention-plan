The playbook, tried on

Nine standard retention plays. Here's what fits Snitch.

Keep

4 plays

  • Smart recommendationsOnly show what's in his size and in stock.
  • Basket cross-sell"Complete the look" a few days after delivery.
  • Full lifecycle flowsBuild post-purchase and exchange first.

Tweak

3 plays
  • Welcome journeyOpen with a 3-question fit quiz, not a discount.

Drop

How I got these numbers
  • Base: FY27 target of ₹1,400 Cr, 60% online (₹840 Cr), 40% stores (₹560 Cr).
  • Stores: 5 / 10 / 20 pts more bills linked; 15% comes back as online repeat in a year.
  • Returns: 1 / 2 / 3 pts lower on online sales.
  • Discount: 1 / 2 / 3 pts lower on ~₹420 Cr of repeat sales.
  • WhatsApp: weekly drop to 1M contacts at ₹0.8631 + GST = ₹5.3 Cr a year, sent to 40 / 20 / 10% instead.
  • All placeholders until Snitch's real data replaces them in week 3.
The first 90 days

Measure, pilot, then scale.

Weeks 1 to 3

Measure

  • Repeat rate by first channel: store, app, Quick
  • Share of store bills linked to a customer
  • Why products come back: fit, fabric, other
Weeks 4 to 8

Pilot

  • E-bill in 10 stores vs 10 control stores
  • Fit quiz on the top 50 products
  • Store exchanges in Bengaluru
Weeks 9 to 13

Prove and scale

  • Access-based SNITCH X, with a holdout
  • Scale only what beat its control
  • Drop what didn't
?

First question on day one: do customers who start in a store come back online as often as those who start on the app? The answer decides which bet goes first.

What could go wrong

And what I'd do about it.

Staff skip capture at the billing rush

Fix: a QR the customer scans himself, and capture rate on the store scorecard.

Sizes differ across styles

Fix: start Fit ID on the top 50 products only.

Members miss their cashback

Fix: test on new members first. Existing members keep theirs.

Numbers I couldn't see

Fix: repeat definition, return rate and discount depth aren't public. Week 3 replaces every estimate.
